Uncomfortable finding from this project I haven't said plainly yet: the cheapest way to heat one of the flats I've been studying isn't gas. It isn't even a perfectly optimised smart storage heater. It's not heating the home at all.

Real prices, same two-bedroom flat:

→ Plug-in radiator + electric blanket: £280/year
→ Gas central heating: £378/year
→ Storage heater, fully optimised: £691/year
→ Storage heater, unmanaged: £927/year

Heating one room with a plug-in radiator, and yourself with an electric blanket at night, is cheaper than every whole-home option on that list — including the smart system I've spent months building. If you're a tenant purely minimising what you spend, that's the rational choice. Not reckless. Not confused. Rational.

The cost that doesn't show up in that table: the room you're not heating stays cold. Cold walls condense moisture. Condensation becomes damp. Damp becomes mould. That's not a chain of assumptions — there's real peer-reviewed research using actual GB smart-meter data on prepayment customers showing people self-disconnect *more* during cold snaps, not less, precisely when they need heat most. Separate survey data: over half of one prepayment sample have drastically cut their energy use recently, and more than a third now describe their home as cold and damp as a direct result.

Here's the part that should concern anyone in social housing specifically: Awaab's Law now makes landlords legally responsible for identifying and fixing exactly that damp and mould, on a fixed statutory clock. Which means a tenant's own rational, cost-minimising response to an unaffordable heating system is precisely the behaviour that creates their landlord's legal exposure. The tenant isn't the problem. The economics are.

That's why I don't think awareness campaigns or "energy saving tips" fix this. Nobody's failing to notice their heater is expensive. They've already done the maths, and £280 beat £927. The only things that actually change the incentive are cheaper running costs (what the smart control in this project targets) or a genuinely different fabric — better insulation, so less heat is needed in the first place.
