The dial calibration is now real — a population-calibrated table built from 42 confirmed storage-heater households' actual 2013 usage (data/intermediate/phase2_index_calibration.parquet), not evenly-spaced guesses. But that source data is from 2013: it predates both the 2021+ energy-price crisis that has driven fuel poverty sharply higher, and Awaab's Law's excess-cold rules for social landlords (from 30 Nov 2026). A 2013 household could afford to charge more freely than many 2026 tenants can — so this calibration may understate real cold-related risk, not overstate it.
The £ savings figures use real electricity rates and a real savings ramp calibrated the same way, but still assume a typical heater size, not your own. They'll get more accurate once we know more about your setup.
Everything shown here — your settings, your recent-nights history — stays on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere except the coordinates needed to fetch the forecast.
